# S.K.E.P.I. v0.1.2-preview — release notes

Body of the GitHub pre-release `v0.1.2-preview` (tag on `@COMMIT12@`). The placeholders `@…@` are
filled by the release steps in `docs/release-process.md` before publishing; the hashes are also in

---

**Project S.K.E.P.I. Developer Preview `v0.1.2-preview`: for developers and testers. Not for emergency use.**

S.K.E.P.I. (Survival Knowledge & Emergency Pocket Intelligence) is an offline knowledge and emergency
app. It offers Wikipedia search and sealed article reading, answers built from source excerpts with an
optional on-device AI summary, maps and places, emergency numbers and tools, and sharing packs between
devices. It has no accounts, analytics or telemetry.

> **Intended purpose.** S.K.E.P.I. is for general reference and education only. It is not a medical
> device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition. Consult a healthcare
> professional for medical advice. In an emergency, call 112 or your local emergency number.

## Verify before installing

```sh
sha256sum -c SHA256SUMS.txt                       # Linux / macOS / Git Bash
Get-FileHash .\SKEPI_0.1.2-preview_x64-setup.exe  # Windows PowerShell; compare with SHA256SUMS.txt
apksigner verify --print-certs skepi-0.1.2-preview-arm64.apk
```

## What changed since 0.1.1-preview

- **Emergency cards with their steps, quoted word for word.** First aid (CPR, severe bleeding, choking,
  burns, fractures, hypothermia, heatstroke, poisoning) from the IFRC International First Aid,
  Resuscitation and Education Guidelines 2025, each step with its section and page (© IFRC, Geneva,
  2025: "Copies of all or part of this study may be made for non-commercial use, providing the source is
  acknowledged."). Water, earthquake, fire and flood from US federal public-domain pages (CDC, FEMA
  Ready.gov) with title, URL and retrieval date and the agencies' no-endorsement statement; passages
  specific to the United States are left out. Every card starts with "Call 112 first" (the country's
  number). 12 cards, 111 steps; a CI check proves each step against its source. No AI writes or
  rewrites card text.
- **No AI summary for medical questions:** cards and source excerpts only.
- **One intended-purpose statement** (above) in the store description, About, README and privacy policy.
- **Map:** your live position and direction while the map is open (accuracy circle, compass heading,
  "My location" to follow, coordinates under the map). Never stored, logged or sent; no new permission.
- **Problem reports:** a shared report that contains an AI summary says "AI-generated summary".
- **About → third-party notices:** IFRC attribution and licence; CDC and FEMA Ready.gov terms and
  no-endorsement statements.
- **F-Droid:** the build's esbuild is compiled from source; the release APK is signed with its byte layout
  preserved so F-Droid can copy the signature (the 0.1.1-preview APK could not be used that way).

## Known limitations

- Developer Preview: tested on a Samsung Galaxy S23 (Android 16) and Windows 11 x64 only. English only.
  Greece map only.
- The card texts are quoted, not adapted: the CDC and Ready.gov passages come from US pages (the US-only
  parts are left out); the bleach dose tables of the CDC page are not included (follow the bleach label).
- Source excerpts are shown verbatim: a wrong or misleading sentence in a pack is shown as written.
- AI summaries can be wrong: check the cited excerpt. Emergency and medical questions get no AI summary.
- Windows installers are unsigned; no automatic updates.
