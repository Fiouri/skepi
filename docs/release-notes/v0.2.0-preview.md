---

**Project S.K.E.P.I. Developer Preview `v0.2.0-preview`: for developers and testers. Not for emergency use.**

S.K.E.P.I. (Survival Knowledge & Emergency Pocket Intelligence) is an offline knowledge and emergency
app. It offers Wikipedia search and sealed article reading, answers built from source excerpts with an
optional on-device AI summary, maps and places for every country, emergency numbers and tools, and
sharing packs between devices. It has no accounts, analytics or telemetry.

> **Intended purpose.** S.K.E.P.I. is for general reference and education only. It is not a medical
> device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition. Consult a healthcare
> professional for medical advice. In an emergency, call 112 or your local emergency number.

## Verify before installing

```sh
sha256sum -c SHA256SUMS.txt                       # Linux / macOS / Git Bash
Get-FileHash .\SKEPI_0.2.0-preview_x64-setup.exe  # Windows PowerShell; compare with SHA256SUMS.txt
apksigner verify --print-certs skepi-0.2.0-preview-arm64.apk
```

## What changed since 0.1.2-preview

- **Maps and places for every country.** Packs → Maps & places lists all 239 countries and territories
  of the signed catalog: search by name, ISO code or a region inside the country, see the download size,
  and download. Nothing is downloaded until you press Download; every file is checked against the signed
  catalog before it opens. One map shows every country you installed; place search and the emergency
  points (hospitals, pharmacies, shelters, water) cover all of them, and the emergency number follows the
  installed country you are in.
- **Parts of large countries.** 19 countries (for example the United States, Canada, Russia, Germany,
  France, Japan) come in map parts of neighbouring regions, each at most about 1.5 GB. Download only the
  part you need, or all parts; a region name ("Bavaria", "Tokyo") finds the part that contains it. The
  country's places come with its first part. Canada's largest provinces use a lower zoom level to stay
  within the size.
- **Emergency numbers from official sources: 35 of 239 countries.** New: Japan (110 police, 119 fire
  and ambulance, 118 coast guard) and Türkiye (112), each with its official source and retrieval date,
  checked like the card sources. Every other country shows **"112 — check the local number"** until an
  official national source is added; no number comes from Wikipedia or other compiled lists.
- **Signed catalog, sequence 4:** 553 packs, 547 of them country map and places packs (141 GB in all,
  mirrored in the `packs-20261024` release). The Greece map and places packs keep their names: an
  installed Greece pack shows an update.
- **Onboarding** offers your own country's packs.

## Known limitations

- Developer Preview: tested on a Samsung Galaxy S23 (Android 16) and Windows 11 x64 only. English only.
- **iOS: downloads are not supported yet** (Maps & places shows the countries; packs are provisioned or
  imported).
- **Several countries at once** were tested on the S23 with up to 3 map packs (Cyprus, Greece and
  Japan's Kanto part) together; phones with less memory and five or more countries are not measured.
- Some parts of countries with overseas territories span areas far apart (for example France's
  Réunion with Provence): "show on map" frames the whole part.
- Emergency numbers: official sources for 35 countries only; elsewhere "112 — check the local number".
- Source excerpts are shown verbatim: a wrong or misleading sentence in a pack is shown as written.
- AI summaries can be wrong: check the cited excerpt. Emergency and medical questions get no AI summary.
- Windows installers are unsigned; no automatic updates.
