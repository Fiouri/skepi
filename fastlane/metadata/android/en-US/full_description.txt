DEVELOPER PREVIEW. NOT FOR EMERGENCY USE. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.

SKEPI (Survival Knowledge & Emergency Pocket Intelligence) is a free, open-source reference library that keeps working without a connection. Download content once, on Wi-Fi, and then read, search and ask questions with no mobile data, no account and no tracking.

WHAT IT DOES

• Offline encyclopedia: search and read Wikipedia articles from Kiwix packs (English top articles and medical articles), in a viewer that runs no scripts and opens no web pages.
• Ask a question: the answer starts with source excerpts, copied word for word from the packs and labelled "not verified advice". An optional summary written by a small AI model on your phone follows; every sentence must match a cited excerpt, or it is not shown. Questions about emergencies get no AI summary.
• Offline maps and places: a map of Greece from OpenStreetMap, with hospitals, pharmacies, shelters and water points, and place search.
• Emergency tools: the emergency number for your country, SOS torch (Morse), compass and a one-shot GPS position.
• Share without the internet: send content packs from phone to phone over Wi-Fi or a local hotspot, paired by QR code, or receive them from the SKEPI desktop app. Every file is checked against a digitally signed catalog before it opens.

IMPORTANT LIMITATIONS

• Not for emergency use. The emergency cards ship without their steps ("Under professional review") until certified first-aid instructors have reviewed them; each card shows the emergency numbers.
• SKEPI is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition. Always consult a healthcare professional for medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.
• Source excerpts are shown as written in the packs, and AI summaries can be wrong. Check the cited source.
• Content is downloaded after installation and is large (about 2 to 8 GB, depending on what you choose). It can also be received from another phone or a computer running SKEPI.
• English only. Map pack: Greece only. Needs a 64-bit (arm64) phone with Android 7.0 or newer. Phones with less than about 3 GB of RAM get source excerpts without AI summaries.

PRIVACY

No accounts, ads, analytics, crash reporting or telemetry. The app uses the internet only for downloads you start, from the hosts listed in the signed catalog (Kiwix, Hugging Face, GitHub). Your questions, answers and location stay on your phone. "Report a problem" prepares a text for you to copy or save; nothing is sent.

PERMISSIONS

• Location (foreground only): your position on the map and in Tools, only when you ask.
• Camera: scanning the pairing QR code of another phone.
• Nearby Wi-Fi devices and Wi-Fi changes: the local hotspot used to share content between phones.
• Internet: downloads you start.
